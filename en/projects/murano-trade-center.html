Murano Trade Center

33-story office tower + 10-story parking ramp

Project Facts

Category Office Tower
Tringe's Role Structural Construction
Location Colombia's Caribbean coast

Murano Trade Center is a large-scale office development: a 33-story office tower paired with a 10-story parking ramp, built on a three-dimensional portal frame system running in two directions.

Building a frame system of this complexity across a tower of this height required precise sequencing and constant quality control — exactly the kind of technically demanding project Tringe's engineering team is built for.
