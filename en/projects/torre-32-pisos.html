32-Story Tower

Reinforced concrete structure + 7-story parking garage — ~16,000 m² built

Project Facts

Category Residential Tower
Built Area ~16,000 m²
Tringe's Role Construction
Location Colombia's Caribbean coast

This residential tower project called for a full reinforced concrete structure spanning 32 floors, paired with a dedicated 7-story parking structure to serve the building — roughly 16,000 m² of structural work in total.

Tringe's team managed the structural execution under PMI-standard project planning, applying strict quality control on concrete placement and reinforcement — the same rigor we bring to every high-rise project.
